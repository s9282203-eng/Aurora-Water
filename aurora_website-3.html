THE VISION

Designed for a world with changing needs.

AURORA is a proposed smart water bottle platform exploring how hydration, thermal control, water treatment and energy-aware design might work together in a portable system.

Concept transparency: The features below are design goals and research directions, not claims of tested performance. Water purification, heating, cooling, atmospheric water generation and energy harvesting require separate engineering, safety testing and validation before real-world use.
FEATURE CONCEPTS

One bottle. Multiple research directions.

Modular ideas that could be investigated through prototyping and technical validation.

❄

Thermal control

Explore insulation and active cooling approaches to help manage beverage temperature.

♨

Heating & treatment

Investigate controlled heating and validated treatment methods with appropriate temperature and safety controls.

◉

Hydration awareness

Explore reminders and simple monitoring features that help users build hydration habits.

💧

Water access research

Study possible purification or collection modules. Safe drinking water would require validated treatment and testing.

ϟ

Energy-aware design

Evaluate battery use, charging and energy budgets before considering energy-harvesting options.

⌘

Smart multi-mode control

Imagine a clear interface for operating supported functions and monitoring system status.

POTENTIAL APPLICATIONS

Built around real-world challenges.

Potential use cases to evaluate — each with different requirements, constraints and safety standards.

🏕️

Outdoor & adventure

Portable hydration concepts for hiking and remote travel.

🛟

Disaster response

Research into dependable water-access tools for emergency settings.

⛰️

High-altitude environments

Consider temperature, pressure, weight and battery constraints.

❄️

Cold-region exploration

Study freeze protection, insulation and controlled thawing concepts.

🌍

Water resilience

Explore responsible design for communities facing water-access challenges.

🚀

Future space missions

A long-term inspiration requiring extensive aerospace engineering and validation.

FROM CONCEPT TO PROTOTYPE

Good ideas need rigorous testing.

AURORA is seeking constructive feedback, engineering expertise, research guidance and potential prototype collaborators. The next step is to define a feasible minimum prototype, estimate power and thermal requirements, and test each subsystem independently.

We welcome conversations with engineers, researchers, product designers and organizations interested in responsible water technology.
